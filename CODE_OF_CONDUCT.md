# Code of Conduct

## Our standard
IdentityMesh aims to maintain a professional, technically rigorous, and respectful collaboration environment.

Contributors should:
- discuss ideas rather than attack people
- give specific technical feedback
- acknowledge uncertainty
- avoid harassment or discrimination
- respect security disclosure practices
- avoid publishing others' sensitive information

## Technical disagreement
Strong disagreement is acceptable.

Prefer:
- evidence
- benchmarks
- threat analysis
- reproducible examples
- design alternatives

Avoid:
- personal attacks
- dismissive language
- status-based arguments

## Security research
Do not use the project community to coordinate unauthorized activity against third-party systems.

## Enforcement
The project maintainer may remove contributions or participation that materially violate these expectations.
